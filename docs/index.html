Residents' essential services in the lens of flood risk: Comparative risk assessment of statistical areas in Turku based on grocery stores

How has the total risk been defined?

Step 1: Risk level of statistical areas based on grocery stores

  1. A risk class for each single grocery store was calculated based on weighted average of flood polygons’ areas within 100 meters buffer around the store
  2. Grocery stores for each statistical area were selected in overlay analysis between area with its 200 meters buffer zone and grocery stores
  3. The final risk level based on grocery stores was an average of single stores’ risk within the area and the buffer zone (there was no risk level for areas without stores)

Step 2: Socio-economic vulnerability of statistical areas in both scenarios

  1. Primary raster data was vectorized, and intersecting grids were defined for each statistical area (every grid that falls even partially in the area)
  2. The vulnerability value is an average of single grid values within the area

Step 3: Total risk of statistical areas

Total risk of statistical areas was calculated as an average of risk level based on grocery stores (Step 1) and vulnerability value (Step 2)
